## English

### Added

- Speed-based field of view: Your view widens as you move faster. The maximum effect is configurable and follows Minecraft's FOV Effects setting.
- Air control setting: You can adjust how strongly you steer while airborne.

### Changes

- Wall run acceleration: Wall runs now build speed gradually to reduce sudden speed changes. The acceleration rate is configurable.
- Sprint jumps: Reduced the extra speed gained from sprint jumps while moving at high speed.
- Sliding: Releasing sprint during a slide now lets you keep sliding as long as you have enough speed.

## 中文

### 新增

- 速度视野效果：移动越快，视野越宽；可调整最大变化幅度，并跟随原版“视场角效果”设置。
- 空中操控配置：可调整空中移动时的转向强度。

### 调整

- 跑墙加速优化：进入跑墙后逐渐提速，减少速度突然变化的感觉；加速快慢可以配置。
- 疾跑跳跃调整：高速移动时，减小疾跑跳跃带来的额外加速。
- 滑铲保持优化：滑铲期间松开疾跑键，只要速度足够，仍可继续滑行。
