## English

### Changes

- Wall run kicks: When turning away from the wall before jumping, the jump follows your movement input. Kicking off while moving along the wall preserves existing speed.
- Jet Booster wall runs: Upward or downward movement gradually settles during a wall run, reducing sudden upward launches.
- Wall kick controls: Direct wall kicks require a more deliberate movement away from the wall. The minimum interval between consecutive kicks is slightly longer.
- Wall run speed: Restored the immediate speed boost on entering a wall run and removed the wall run acceleration setting.

### Fixes

- Wall run speed: Fixed speed increasing abnormally throughout a wall run.

## 中文

### 调整

- 跑墙墙跳优化：转向墙外起跳时，按玩家的移动输入选择跳跃方向；沿墙起跳时保留已有速度。
- 助推器跑墙优化：进入跑墙后逐渐停止上升或下落，减少突然向上弹起的情况。
- 墙跳操作调整：直接蹬墙跳需要更明确地朝墙外移动，并适当延长连续起跳的最短间隔。
- 跑墙提速调整：恢复进入跑墙时直接补足速度，并移除跑墙加速快慢的配置项。

### 修复

- 跑墙速度修复：修复持续跑墙时速度不断异常增加的问题。
