## English

### Changes

- Automatic step-down fix: Fixed automatic step-down sometimes skipping multiple steps at once.
- Stair tops: Fixed high-speed movement being blocked by the last step when no platform extends beyond it.
- Entity pushing: In Momentum mode, players and entities that block movement, such as boats, no longer push each other unless the player is sneaking.

## 中文

### 调整

- 自动下坡修复：修复自动下坡时可能一次跨过多级台阶的问题。
- 楼梯顶端通过修复：修复楼梯顶端没有延伸平台时，高速移动会被最后一级挡住的问题。
- 实体推挤调整：机动模式下默认不再与船等会阻挡移动的实体互相推挤，潜行时恢复正常。
