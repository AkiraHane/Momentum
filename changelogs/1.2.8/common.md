## English

### Changes

- Wall kick speed: Wall kicks preserve existing horizontal speed more reliably, reducing sudden slowdowns when changing direction.
- Chaining wall runs: It is easier to continue a wall run on another wall after kicking off the first one.

## 中文

### 调整

- 墙跳速度优化：蹬墙跳时更好地保留已有的水平速度，减少转向时突然减速的情况。
- 连续跑墙优化：从跑墙中跳开后，更容易接上下一面墙继续跑墙。
