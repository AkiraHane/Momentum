## English

### Changes

- Speed-based field of view: Movement-related FOV widening is now off by default and can be enabled in the config.
- Config translations: Added Chinese and English descriptions for air control, wall run acceleration, and FOV settings.

### Fixes

- Vanilla mode visuals: The vanilla camera and held-item view are restored when maneuver mode is disabled or the player enters a vanilla movement state, such as flying or gliding with an elytra.

## 中文

### 调整

- 速度视野效果调整：默认关闭移动时的视野加宽，可在配置中开启。
- 配置翻译补充：为空中操控、跑墙加速和视野变化设置补齐中英文说明。

### 修复

- 原版模式显示修复：关闭机动模式或进入飞行、鞘翅滑翔等原版状态后，恢复原版相机和持物显示。
