## English

### Changes

- Dodge controls: By default, hold sprint and double-tap a direction key to dodge. The config also offers holding a direction key and pressing or double-tapping sprint.

### Fixes

- Jump animations: Added missing animations for airborne jumps and wall kicks, and fixed some jumps keeping the sliding pose.

## 中文

### 调整

- 闪避操作调整：默认改为按住疾跑键并双击方向键触发；也可在配置中启用按住方向键、单击或双击疾跑键触发的方式。

### 修复

- 跳跃动画修复：补齐空中起跳和蹬墙跳时的动画，修复部分跳跃仍保持滑铲姿势的问题。
