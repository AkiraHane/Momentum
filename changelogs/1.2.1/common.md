## English

### First Stable Release

- Ground movement: Hold the lower-center key (default `C`) to slide when moving fast enough or crawl at low speed. Hold it while falling to prepare a landing roll and reduce fall damage.
- Wall interaction: Wall run, climb, slide, hang, and kick off walls, or vault onto platforms and into low gaps from a ledge.
- Power jump: Release sneak and immediately jump to gain extra height and distance.
- Dodge: Hold a direction key and double-tap sprint to move quickly in that direction. Equipping the Jet Booster also allows mid-air dodges.
- Swim dash: Hold forward and press sprint underwater to dash in your look direction. Dashing out of the water starts a dolphin jump.
- Jet Booster: Equip it in the leggings slot or a Curios belt slot to improve movement and jumping and reduce fall damage.
- Mode and key hints: Use `Shift + M` to toggle maneuver mode and `Shift + N` to toggle hints for available actions by default.

### Fixes

- Logging: Reduced debug log output when switching actions.

## 中文

### 首次正式发布

- 地面机动：速度足够时按降低重心键（默认 `C`）滑铲，低速时匍匐；下落时按住可准备受身，减轻摔落伤害。
- 墙面交互：支持跑墙、爬墙、滑墙、挂墙、蹬墙跳，以及从边缘翻上平台或钻入矮洞。
- 蓄力跳：松开潜行键后立即起跳，可以跳得更高、更远。
- 闪避：按住方向键并双击疾跑键，向对应方向快速移动；装备喷气助推器后可在空中使用。
- 水中推进：在水下按住前进并按下疾跑键，向视线方向冲刺；冲出水面时进入海豚跳。
- 喷气助推器：可装备在护腿槽或 Curios 腰带槽，提升移动和跳跃能力，并减轻摔落伤害。
- 模式与按键提示：默认使用 `Shift + M` 切换机动模式，`Shift + N` 切换当前可用动作的按键提示。

### 修复

- 日志输出优化：减少动作切换时产生的调试日志。
