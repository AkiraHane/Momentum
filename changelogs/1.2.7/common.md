## English

### Fixes

- Sliding on slopes: Fixed slide acceleration pointing in the wrong direction on some steps and slopes.
- Ladder acceleration: Fixed abnormal upward acceleration after releasing forward or reaching the top of a ladder.
- Dolphin jump transitions: Exiting a dolphin jump now restores the normal pose and improves transitions to wall actions such as ladder climbing.

## 中文

### 修复

- 坡面滑铲修复：修复部分台阶、坡面上的滑铲加速方向不正确的问题。
- 爬梯加速修复：修复松开前进键或到达梯顶后仍异常向上加速的问题。
- 海豚跳衔接修复：退出海豚跳后恢复正常姿态，改善衔接爬梯等墙面动作时无法正常切换的问题。
